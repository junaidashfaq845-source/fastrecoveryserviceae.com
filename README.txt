FAST RECOVERY SERVICES - WEBSITE (fastrecoveryserviceae.com)

HOW TO UPLOAD
1. Unzip this folder.
2. Upload EVERYTHING inside it to your hosting "public_html" (or "www") folder.
   index.html must sit directly in public_html, with the images folder next to it.
3. Open https://fastrecoveryserviceae.com/ and check the page on your phone.

FILES
index.html            the website
images/               all photos and the logo
favicon.png           browser tab icon
apple-touch-icon.png  phone home-screen icon
robots.txt            tells Google it may crawl the site
sitemap.xml           list of pages for Google

AFTER GOING LIVE
- Turn on SSL / HTTPS in your hosting panel.
- Add the site in Google Search Console and submit https://fastrecoveryserviceae.com/sitemap.xml
- Create and verify the Google Business Profile with the same phone number.
- Turn on auto-renew for the domain.
